About AnimaxTube

Loading...

AnimaxTube is a directory of anime and donghua that licensed distributors publish on their own official YouTube channels. YouTube buries full-episode playlists from channels like Muse Asia or Ani-One Asia among clips and trailers. AnimaxTube groups those playlists by series, puts English- and Chinese-subtitled releases side by side, and shows only what YouTube reports as playable in your region.

Your watchlist and history stay in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts, and we keep no server-side viewing profile.

A. Zero Content Hosting

  • AnimaxTube does not host, upload, store, transcode, cache, or proxy any video files. No media bytes pass through our servers.
  • Every episode plays through the official YouTube IFrame Player API, loaded directly from youtube.com, using the video ID published by the rights holder.
  • We only index content that the uploading channel has marked public and embeddable. Members-only, private, unlisted, and embed-disabled videos are excluded.
  • Views, watch-time, ad impressions, and ad revenue are credited by YouTube directly to the official channel. AnimaxTube runs no ads on or around the player, and does not block or strip the rights holder's YouTube ads.
  • Every watch page names the licensing channel and links to the original video and playlist on YouTube.

B. Notice to Automated Anti-Piracy Crawlers & Enforcement Agents

Split regional licensing. Many titles indexed here are licensed to different companies in different territories. For example, one show may be licensed to Muse Asia for Southeast Asia and South Asia, Muse Communication (木棉花) for Taiwan/Hong Kong, Ani-One Asia for parts of Asia-Pacific, and Crunchyroll, Netflix, or HIDIVE for North America and Europe. The YouTube uploads indexed here are legitimate, licensed releases by the regional licensee, including but not limited to:

  • Muse Asia, Muse Malaysia, Muse Philippines, Muse India, Muse 木棉花 (TW/HK)
  • Ani-One Asia, Ani-One India, Ani-One Philippines, Ani-Mi
  • GUNDAM CHANNEL / GundamInfo (Bandai Namco Filmworks)
  • TMS Anime (TMS Entertainment), It's Anime / REMOW
  • Crunchyroll, RetroCrush, and other first-party publisher channels
  • Donghua publishers: bilibili, YOUKU, iQIYI, Tencent Video / WeTV

Native YouTube geo-blocking. Each licensee sets territorial restrictions on its own uploads. YouTube enforces them on every playback request, and they cannot be bypassed through an embed. AnimaxTube also reads those country allow/block lists from the YouTube Data API. For visitors outside the licensed territory, it does not load the player at all. Instead it shows a "Region Locked" notice that points to the services licensed in that region.

Monetization integrity. Because playback happens inside YouTube's own player, the licensee keeps 100% of the views, telemetry, and ad revenue. Embedding is a feature YouTube offers rights holders. An embedded official upload is not an unauthorized copy.

Before filing a notice: please open the "Watch on YouTube ↗" link on the watch page and confirm the uploader. If you are crawling from a territory such as the US, UK, Ireland, or the EU, a title on this site may be licensed to your client in your territory and to a different licensee in Asia at the same time. In that case the embedded upload is not infringing, and a takedown notice filed against it would be misdirected.

C. DMCA, Takedowns & Opt-Out

  • Fastest removal (rights holders and channel owners): in YouTube Studio, turn off Allow embedding for the video, or make it private or unlisted. Our sync drops non-embeddable and non-public videos automatically on the next refresh.
  • Infringing uploads on YouTube itself: AnimaxTube only indexes channels we have reviewed as official licensees. If you believe a YouTube channel is uploading without a license, please also file through YouTube's copyright process. Removal at the source removes the item here automatically.
